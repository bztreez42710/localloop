REFUNDS & CANCELLATIONS

Refund and cancellation policy

Customers may cancel an unclaimed delivery or shopping request through the app. Once a driver has accepted or begun work, cancellation may be limited and reasonable earned driver compensation or nonrefundable third-party costs may still apply where permitted by law.

Shopping orders

Merchandise spending is limited to the amount the customer authorizes. If the actual receipt is less than the funded merchandise amount, the unused amount should be returned or released through the payment processor. If a shopper cannot buy an item, the customer should not be charged for that item. Substitutions should follow customer instructions.

Failed or incomplete service

If a delivery is not completed, is materially different from the accepted request, or contains a verified billing error, LocalLoop may issue a full or partial refund after review. Refund timing depends on the payment provider and bank.

Store returns

Returns of merchandise bought from an unaffiliated store are subject to that store's return policy. LocalLoop does not guarantee that a store will accept a return.

Disputes

Users should report billing, delivery, or safety issues promptly with order details and available evidence. LocalLoop may temporarily hold internal settlement records while investigating a dispute. Nothing in this policy limits nonwaivable consumer rights.
