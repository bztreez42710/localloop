PROHIBITED ITEMS

Items LocalLoop will not transport

During the Spokane pilot, LocalLoop prohibits requests involving cannabis or marijuana products, illegal drugs, controlled substances without a lawful specialized program, alcohol unless LocalLoop later implements a Washington LCB-approved contractor arrangement, tobacco or nicotine products, firearms or ammunition, explosives, hazardous materials requiring regulated transport, stolen goods, counterfeit goods, cash or cash-equivalent transfers, pornography or unlawful sexual-content transactions, live animals, human remains, or any item whose possession, purchase, transport, or delivery would violate law.

Prescription medication delivery is disabled unless LocalLoop later establishes a compliant pharmacy/medical-delivery program. Drivers must refuse any request they reasonably believe contains a prohibited or unsafe item and should report the request through LocalLoop support.
