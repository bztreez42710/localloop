OWNER PAYOUT SETTINGS

Where LocalLoop money should go.

Customer payments are processed by PayPal. For security, LocalLoop does not store your full bank routing or account number. You enter those directly with PayPal; LocalLoop only stores your payout preference and masked bank information.

Accepted customer payments

LocalLoop is set up around PayPal checkout so customers can be shown PayPal-supported methods such as PayPal, major credit/debit cards, Venmo, digital wallets, and eligible bank-transfer options. What a buyer sees depends on PayPal eligibility and the buyer's device/account.

Paper checks are not accepted.
