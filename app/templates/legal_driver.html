Independent Driver Agreement

This agreement governs access to LocalLoop by independent drivers and shoppers during the Spokane pilot.

Independent business relationship

Drivers decide whether, when, and where to make themselves available and which offered requests to accept. Drivers may use other platforms or serve their own customers, subject to completing accepted LocalLoop jobs safely and lawfully. This agreement does not by itself determine legal worker classification; Washington and federal law control.

Driver responsibilities

Drivers are responsible for a valid driver license, lawful vehicle registration, safe vehicle condition, compliance with traffic laws, taxes applicable to their earnings, and maintaining insurance that actually covers delivery/gig activity. Drivers must promptly report any lapse or material change in eligibility or coverage.

Verification

LocalLoop may require identity, driving-record, background, and insurance review. Any third-party consumer report will be handled using required notices, authorization, and adverse-action procedures where the Fair Credit Reporting Act or other law applies.

Pay and expenses

Before acceptance, LocalLoop aims to disclose the offered driver/shopper pay. Unless a separate written policy says otherwise, drivers are responsible for their own vehicle, fuel, phone, maintenance, taxes, and ordinary operating costs. Merchandise purchased for a funded shopping order is reimbursable only up to the customer-authorized amount and requires an accurate receipt.

Safety

No accepted job requires unsafe driving or violation of law. Drivers must not handle prohibited items, falsify receipts or proof, share accounts, misuse customer information, or drive while impaired or distracted.

Deactivation and disputes

LocalLoop may suspend access for fraud, safety threats, invalid credentials, insurance lapse, unlawful conduct, or material breach. Where practical, drivers may request review of a deactivation decision. Nothing here waives rights that cannot legally be waived.
