Independent Shopper & Driver Agreement

This agreement governs voluntary access to LocalLoop's Spokane marketplace by independent shoppers and delivery providers.

Your choice to work

You decide whether to use LocalLoop, when to go online, where to operate within the supported service area, and whether to accept or pass on each request shown to you. There is no required schedule, minimum acceptance rate, minimum number of jobs, exclusivity requirement, or guaranteed amount of work. You may use other platforms and serve your own customers.

Accepted requests

Once you voluntarily accept a request, you agree to make a reasonable effort to complete that specific request safely, lawfully, and according to the customer's authorized instructions. Marketplace safety rules, fraud controls and requirements for an accepted job do not create a promise of employment or guaranteed earnings. Applicable law determines worker classification regardless of terminology used here.

Your business decisions and expenses

Before acceptance, LocalLoop aims to display the offered compensation and useful job information. You decide whether that offer makes economic sense for you. Unless law or a separate written policy requires otherwise, you are responsible for your own vehicle, fuel, phone, maintenance, taxes and ordinary operating expenses. Merchandise bought for a funded shopping request is reimbursable only under the order's authorized budget and receipt rules.

Licensing, vehicle and insurance

You are responsible for maintaining a valid driver license when driving is involved, lawful registration, a safe vehicle, compliance with traffic laws, and insurance that actually covers the activity you perform. You must report material lapses or changes.

Verification and privacy

LocalLoop may require proportionate identity, driving-record, background and insurance verification for marketplace safety. Third-party consumer reports, when used, must follow applicable disclosure, authorization and adverse-action requirements.

Safety and independence

No LocalLoop request requires unsafe driving or violation of law. You must not handle prohibited items, falsify receipts or proof, share accounts, misuse customer information, steal, commit fraud, or drive impaired or dangerously distracted. You retain discretion over lawful methods and routing for completing an accepted request except for customer requirements inherent to that request.

Account access

LocalLoop may restrict access for fraud, credible safety threats, invalid credentials, insurance lapse, unlawful conduct or material breach. Where practical, providers may request review. Nothing in this agreement waives rights or obligations that applicable law does not permit the parties to waive.
