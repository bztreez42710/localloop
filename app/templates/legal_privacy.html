LocalLoop Privacy Policy

LocalLoop collects only information reasonably needed to operate the Spokane delivery marketplace, prevent fraud, support users, meet legal obligations, and improve safety.

Information we collect

Account information may include name, email, phone, role, business name, delivery addresses, order details, approximate or live driver location while a driver chooses to share it for an active delivery, receipt or delivery photos, support/dispute records, device/session information, and records showing which legal terms were accepted. Driver compliance records may include approval status and limited insurance information. Sensitive payment credentials should be sent directly to the regulated payment provider and should not be stored by LocalLoop.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, match and complete deliveries, calculate routes and fees, communicate order status, process payments and refunds, investigate safety issues, prevent fraud, maintain accounting records, and comply with law.

Sharing

Information may be shared with users as needed to complete an order, with payment processors, hosting and communications providers, background-screening or insurance-verification providers when lawfully engaged, professional advisers, and authorities when legally required. LocalLoop does not sell personal information for advertising.

Retention and security

LocalLoop aims to minimize data collection and keep data only as long as reasonably needed for operations, tax/accounting, disputes, safety, and legal requirements. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards are used. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Location and photos

Driver location is used only for service-area checks, routing, and active-order tracking. Delivery and receipt photos should not intentionally capture unrelated people, private documents, payment cards, medical information, or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Data incidents

If Washington law requires breach notification, LocalLoop will provide legally required notices within the applicable time limits.

Children

LocalLoop is not intended for children under 13. Driver accounts are for adults legally able to enter contracts and drive.

Your choices

Users may request correction or deletion of information that LocalLoop is not legally required to keep. Some records may be retained for fraud prevention, accounting, disputes, or legal compliance.
