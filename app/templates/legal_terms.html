Two marketplace models

Participating Marketplace Stores voluntarily create a LocalLoop presence and are responsible for their own listings, products, prices, inventory, taxes and legal obligations as applicable. Personal Shopping Requests are customer-created errands asking an independent shopper to visit a customer-selected public Spokane retailer. An unaffiliated retailer is not represented as a LocalLoop participant.

Independent providers
